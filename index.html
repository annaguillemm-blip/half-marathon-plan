How to use the calorie targets

  • They're floors, not ceilings: hungry on a hard day? Eat.
  • Protein 90–110 g a day. Carbs are what rise on KEY and long-run days.
  • Snack within an hour after training, and move 300–400 kcal from dinner into the day.
  • Office on Tuesday? Move upper body to Monday and keep Friday as rest. Calories follow the training, not the weekday.

Why your weight trend won't jump around

Busy and quiet walking days even out over the week. Judge weight and waist on the weekly average. If you walk a lot more for several weeks in a row, use the calculator so the extra is covered.

Move toward the higher end if you notice

  • Afternoon heart-rate dips coming back, or runs feeling flat.
  • Gym weights dropping, poorer sleep, or resting heart rate creeping up.
  • Any change in your cycle, or weight or waist trending down for 2–3 weeks.
